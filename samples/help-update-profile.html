Update your profile

Updated 12 November 2026 · 4 min read · Applies to: all users

Your profile holds your personal details, preferences and payout information. Keeping it up to date means your channel manager can reach you, your payouts arrive on time, and Tandem shows dates and amounts the way you expect.

Tip: A completeness indicator at the top of your profile lists anything that is missing. Select an item to go straight to the right section.

Open your profile

Do one of the following:

  • On the home page, select the Profile tile.
  • Select your name in the top-right corner, then select View profile.

Your profile opens with a menu of sections on the left.

What you can change in each section

SectionWhat it contains
Personal detailsYour name, job title, email address and phone numbers.
PreferencesDisplay language, time zone, date format and currency.
NotificationsWhich emails Tandem sends you, such as deal updates and the weekly summary.
Payout detailsThe bank account your commission payouts are paid into. Only visible if you are your company's finance contact.
DelegatesColleagues who can register deals or view commissions on your behalf.
DevicesMobile devices signed in to your account.
Change historyA record of every change made to your profile, and who made it.

Update your personal details

  1. In the profile menu, select Personal details.
  2. Select Edit next to the information you want to change.
  3. Make your changes, then select Save.
Important: If you change your email address, Tandem sends a confirmation link to the new address. The change only takes effect after you select the link. Until then, keep using your old address to sign in.

Set your language, time zone and currency

  1. In the profile menu, select Preferences.
  2. Choose your Language, Time zone, Date format and Currency.
  3. Select Update.

The currency you choose is used to display amounts. It does not change the currency your payouts are made in, which is set in your partner agreement.

Add or change your payout details

This section is only available to your company's finance contact.

  1. In the profile menu, select Payout details.
  2. Select Add bank account, or Edit next to the existing account.
  3. Enter the account holder name and bank details, then select Save.
  4. Enter the verification code sent to your email address.
Important: Changes to payout details take two working days to be verified. Payouts scheduled during that time are paid into the previous account.

Let a colleague act on your behalf

  1. In the profile menu, select Delegates.
  2. Select Add delegate and search for your colleague by name or email address.
  3. Choose what they can do: Register deals, View commissions, or both.
  4. Select Add.

Your colleague must already have a Tandem account at your company. To remove a delegate, select Remove next to their name.

Remove a device

If you lose a phone or stop using a device, remove it so it can no longer access your account.

  1. In the profile menu, select Devices.
  2. Next to the device, select Remove, then confirm.

See what has changed

Select Change history in the profile menu, or View change history at the top of any section. Each entry shows the date, the field that changed, the old and new values, and who made the change. Bank account numbers are partly hidden.

Troubleshooting

A field is greyed out and I can't edit it

Some fields are managed by your company, for example your job title if it comes from your HR system. A message appears when you select the field. Contact your Tandem administrator to change it.

I don't see the Payout details section

Only your company's finance contact can see payout details. Ask your Tandem administrator who the finance contact is, or to give you this role.

I didn't receive the email confirmation link

Check your spam folder. The link is valid for 24 hours. To get a new one, go back to Personal details and select Resend confirmation.
