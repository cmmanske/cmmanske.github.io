Advance notice

[SECURITY] Single sign-on becomes mandatory for partner users

Made forPartner users · Partner managers · Administrators
Activation required?Yes, before 1 March 2027
Admin ConsoleAdmin Console › Security › Authentication
Effective dateTandem 4.4 (1 March 2027)

Synopsis

From Tandem 4.4, partner users will no longer be able to sign in with an email address and password. They will sign in through single sign-on (SSO) with their own company's identity provider, or with a one-time sign-in link sent by email. This reduces the risk of shared and reused passwords on accounts that can see your deal and commission data.

What will happen?

  • Now to 28 February 2027: nothing changes for partner users. Administrators can turn on SSO early and test it with a pilot group.
  • From 1 March 2027: password sign-in is removed for partner users. Partners without SSO receive a one-time sign-in link each time they sign in from a new device.
ImportantPartner users who still sign in with a password on 1 March 2027 will be asked to verify their email address at their next sign-in. Tell your partners in advance to avoid calls to your support team.

What do you need to do?

  1. Go to Admin Console › Security › Authentication.
  2. Under Partner sign-in, select Configure SSO and enter your identity provider details.
  3. Select a pilot group of partners and choose Enable for selected partners.
  4. When the pilot is successful, choose Enable for all partners.

Partner Portal

[DEAL REGISTRATION] Duplicate deal detection at registration

Made forPartner users · Channel managers
Activation required?No, on by default
Admin ConsoleAdmin Console › Deals › Registration rules (to adjust matching)

Synopsis

When two partners register the same opportunity, channel managers spend time resolving the conflict, and one partner is often disappointed. Tandem now checks for possible duplicates while the partner is completing the deal registration form, before the deal is submitted.

How does it work?

When a partner enters the customer's company name and domain, Tandem compares them with open deals registered in the last 90 days. If it finds a possible match, a message appears below the Customer field:

Message shown to the partner“A deal for this customer may already be registered. You can still submit this registration. Your channel manager will review it.”

The partner can still submit. The registration then appears in the channel manager's Deal review queue with a Possible duplicate label and a link to the matching deal. The partner never sees the other partner's name or deal details.

NoteAdministrators can change the matching period (30, 60, 90 or 180 days) or turn off domain matching in Admin Console › Deals › Registration rules.

[ONBOARDING] Configurable onboarding checklists

Made forPartner managers · New partner users
Activation required?Yes, create at least one checklist
Admin ConsoleAdmin Console › Onboarding › Checklists

Synopsis

Partner managers can now build onboarding checklists for each partner tier, so new partners know exactly what to do in their first weeks: sign the agreement, complete training, add team members, register a first deal. New partner users see their checklist on the portal home page until every item is complete.

How do I create a checklist?

  1. Go to Admin Console › Onboarding › Checklists and select New checklist.
  2. Choose the partner tiers the checklist applies to.
  3. Add items. Each item can link to a page in the portal, a training course or an external URL.
  4. Mark items that Tandem can complete automatically (for example, Register your first deal).
  5. Select Publish.

Partner managers can follow each partner's progress in the Onboarding column of the partner list.

NotePublishing a checklist does not affect partners who joined before the publication date, unless you select Also assign to existing partners.

[PARTNER DIRECTORY] Filter the partner directory by tier and region

Made forChannel managers · Sales teams
Activation required?No

Synopsis

The partner directory now has Tier and Region filters, so sales teams can quickly find a certified partner to bring into a deal. Filters can be combined, and the selected filters are kept in the page URL so they can be shared with colleagues.

Commissions

[PAYOUTS] Downloadable payout statements

Made forPartner finance contacts · Finance administrators
Activation required?No

Synopsis

Partners often need a document for their own accounting when they receive a commission payment. Each payout now has a statement that partners can download as a PDF from Commissions › Payouts.

What is in the statement?

  • Payout reference, payment date and currency
  • Each deal included, with the deal value, commission rate and commission amount
  • Adjustments (for example, clawbacks on cancelled deals), each with a reason
  • Total paid
NoteStatements are available for payouts made from 1 January 2026. Statements are generated in the partner's payout currency.

Platform

[USER PROFILE] Profile completeness indicator

Made forAll users
Activation required?No

Synopsis

Incomplete profiles cause problems later: payouts fail without bank details, and channel managers cannot reach partners without a phone number. A completeness indicator now appears at the top of the profile page and lists the missing information. Each item links directly to the section where it can be added.

For more information, see the Help Center article Update your profile.

[ADMIN CONSOLE] Export the audit log

Made forAdministrators
Activation required?No
Admin ConsoleAdmin Console › Security › Audit log

Synopsis

Administrators can now export the audit log as a CSV file for a selected date range (up to 12 months). This makes it easier to answer security questionnaires and to keep a record outside Tandem.

ImportantThe export contains user names and email addresses. Store it in line with your company's data protection policy.
